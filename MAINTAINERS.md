## Rules

- Curation decisions (add, move, remove an entry) need approval from two maintainers.
- A maintainer never approves changes to entries for an organization they work for, advise, or invest in.
- Affiliations are listed above and kept current.
- When a maintainer joins or leaves, update `CONDUCT_MAINTAINERS` in `.github/workflows/conduct.yml` in the same pull request.
