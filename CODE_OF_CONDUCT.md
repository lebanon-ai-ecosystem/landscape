# Code of conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Reporting a concern

There is no reporting email address. Reports start with a GitHub issue that says only that you want to talk:

1. Open a [Conduct concern](https://github.com/lebanon-ai-ecosystem/landscape/issues/new?template=conduct.yml) issue. Any issue with "CoC" in its title works the same way.
2. Do not describe what happened or name anyone. The issue is public; it only tells the maintainers to contact you.
3. A workflow labels the issue, notifies the maintainers, and locks it so nobody else can comment.
4. Within 3 days a maintainer contacts you privately, in the way you chose on the form:
   - **Private repository.** The maintainer creates a private repository for your report alone and invites you. Only you and the maintainers handling the report can see it. It is deleted when the report is closed, unless you ask to keep it.
   - **Your GitHub profile.** The maintainer uses the contact details on your public profile.
5. The maintainer closes the public issue once the private conversation has started. Closing it says nothing about the outcome.

A maintainer who is involved in the matter, or who works for, advises or invests in an organization involved, does not handle the report. Say on the form if the concern involves a maintainer.

If you have no GitHub account, ask someone you trust to open the issue on your behalf; the maintainer will arrange contact through them.

## What happens next

The maintainer handling the report decides on a response in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, tells you the outcome, and keeps your identity confidential unless you agree otherwise.
